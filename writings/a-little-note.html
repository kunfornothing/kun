A Little Note

For someone who may not know how much they mean to me.

I guess if you’re reading this, you actually opened the folder.

I wanted to leave one more little birthday wish here.

We don’t really know each other that well yet, but I’m genuinely glad that TikTok’s algorithm brought you to my feed.

Somewhere between your stories, and little moments, I started thinking, “Oh, I really like this person…”

And somehow, that little thought stayed.

Maybe it’s the way you see things. Maybe it’s your creativity. Maybe it’s simply the way you seem to be yourself without trying too hard.

I don’t really know.

I just know that I’m happy I found you.

So, wherever you are, and whatever this year brings you, I hope there will be many little things that make you smile.

Happy birthday.

— Kun
